# Privacy Policy for Tailwind

**Last Updated: January 2025**

## Overview

Tailwind ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

## Information We Collect

### Location Data
- **What**: GPS coordinates during rides
- **Why**: To track your route, calculate distance, and provide elevation data
- **Storage**: Stored locally on your device only
- **Sharing**: Never shared with third parties

### Health & Fitness Data
- **What**: Heart rate, calories burned, ride statistics
- **Why**: To track your fitness progress and sync with HealthKit
- **Storage**: Stored locally on your device and in your iCloud Health data (if HealthKit sync is enabled)
- **Sharing**: Never shared with third parties

### Sensor Data
- **What**: Speed, cadence, heart rate from Bluetooth sensors
- **Why**: To provide real-time ride metrics
- **Storage**: Stored locally on your device only
- **Sharing**: Never shared with third parties

### User Preferences
- **What**: App settings, bike configurations, maintenance records
- **Why**: To personalize your experience
- **Storage**: Stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults
- **Sharing**: Never shared with third parties

## Data We DO NOT Collect

- ❌ No analytics or tracking
- ❌ No advertising identifiers
- ❌ No crash reports sent to external services
- ❌ No account registration or login required
- ❌ No cloud backup (unless you use iCloud backup for your device)
- ❌ No sharing with third parties

## How We Use Your Data

All data is used exclusively for app functionality:
- Displaying your current ride metrics
- Storing ride history
- Tracking bike maintenance schedules
- Syncing workouts to Apple Health (if enabled)
- Detecting segments and comparing performance

## Data Storage

- **Local Only**: All data is stored locally on your iPhone/iPad
- **iCloud Backup**: If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device, your app data may be included in your device backup
- **HealthKit**: If you enable HealthKit integration, workout data is stored in Apple Health according to Apple's Health privacy policy

## Data Security

- All data remains on your device
- Standard iOS app sandboxing protects your data
- No transmission of data to external servers
- No account credentials to protect (no login required)

## Your Rights

You own your data and can:
- Delete individual rides from ride history
- Clear all app data by deleting the app
- Disable location access in iOS Settings
- Disable HealthKit access in iOS Settings
- Export ride data (GPX export coming soon)

## Children's Privacy

Tailwind does not knowingly collect information from children. The app is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages, but parental supervision is recommended for children using fitness tracking features.

## Third-Party Services

Tailwind does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app uses only Apple's native frameworks:
- CoreLocation (for GPS)
- CoreBluetooth (for sensors)
- HealthKit (for workout sync)
- MapKit (for map display)

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in the app and on our GitHub repository.

## Data Deletion

To delete all your data:
1. Delete individual rides from the Ride History screen, or
2. Delete the Tailwind app from your device

To delete HealthKit data:
1. Open Apple Health app
2. Navigate to Browse > Activity > Workouts
3. Find Tailwind workouts and delete them

## California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected
- Know whether personal information is sold or disclosed
- Say no to the sale of personal information
- Access your personal information

**Tailwind does not sell your personal information. All data remains on your device.**

## European Users (GDPR)

Under GDPR, you have the right to:
- Access your data (view in app)
- Rectify your data (edit in app)
- Erase your data (delete rides or delete app)
- Data portability (export coming soon)

**Tailwind processes data locally on your device only. No data is transferred to servers or third parties.**
