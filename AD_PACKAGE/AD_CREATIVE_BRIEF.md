# Tailwind - 15 Second Ad Creative Brief

## Product Overview
**Tailwind** - Pro cycling computer app for iOS that tracks rides, races segments, monitors fitness, and manages bike maintenance.

---

## Ad Concept: "Your Complete Cycling Companion"

### Target Audience
- Serious recreational cyclists (road, gravel, mountain)
- Ages 25-50
- Currently using basic tracking apps or considering a bike computer
- Value performance tracking and data

### Key Message
Tailwind gives you everything a $500 Garmin does, right on your iPhone - plus features they don't offer.

---

## 15-Second Script

### Version A: "The Complete Package"
```
[0-3s] VISUAL: iPhone mounted on handlebars, cyclist riding scenic route
       AUDIO: "Meet Tailwind."

[3-8s] VISUAL: Quick cuts showing app features:
       - Real-time speed/distance on screen
       - GPS trail with gradient colors on map
       - Segment detection indicator popping up
       - Training load charts
       AUDIO: "Your complete cycling computer. Track rides, race segments, monitor fitness."

[8-12s] VISUAL: Split screen - Garmin Edge ($500) vs iPhone with Tailwind
        - Show similar features side by side
        - Maintenance tracking feature (Tailwind exclusive)
        AUDIO: "Everything a Garmin does, plus bike maintenance tracking."

[12-15s] VISUAL: Tailwind app icon, "Available on TestFlight"
         AUDIO: "Tailwind. Your ride, elevated."
```

### Version B: "Feature Focus"
```
[0-2s] VISUAL: Cyclist clipping in, iPhone on handlebars
       AUDIO: "Every ride. Every detail."

[2-6s] VISUAL: Fast montage:
       - GPS breadcrumb trail drawing on map
       - Mile markers appearing
       - Auto-pause indicator
       - Heart rate zones
       AUDIO: "GPS tracking. Auto-pause. Heart rate zones."

[6-10s] VISUAL: Segment detection screen
        - "Downtown Sprint" segment activates

         - Chain: 1,800 of 2,000 miles

         AUDIO: "Tailwind. Pro cycling, simplified."
```

## Visual Style Guide

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Sunset gradient (Orange #FF6B35 → Purple #6B4E9C)
- **Secondary**: Cycling greens/blues for speed heatmap
  - Green (#4CAF50) - Fast sections
  - Cyan (#00BCD4) - Very fast
  - Red/Orange - Slower sections
- **Background**: Dark/black for high contrast
- **Accent**: White text for readability

### Cinematography Style
- **Lighting**: Golden hour / sunset lighting (matches app theme)
- **Camera**: Dynamic, energetic
  - POV shots from handlebars
  - Close-ups of app interface
  - Drone shots of cyclist on scenic route
- **Pacing**: Fast cuts, high energy (music-video style)
- **Setting**: Mix of urban and scenic
  - City streets with lights (auto-pause feature)
  - Mountain/hill roads (elevation tracking)
  - Sunrise/sunset ride (beautiful visuals)

Provide these app screen recordings for overlay:

1. **Main ride screen (landscape mode)**
   - Speed: 18.5 mph
   - Distance: 12.3 mi
   - Duration: 00:42:15
   - Heart rate: 152 bpm
   - Map showing gradient trail

2. **Segment detection popup**
   - "Downtown Sprint"
   - Time: 00:03:42
   - Delta: -00:08 vs PR (green, showing ahead)

3. **Speed heatmap view**
   - Route with color-coded sections
   - Mile markers visible
   - Start marker with bike icon

4. **Training load chart**
   - CTL/ATL/TSB graph
   - 30-day trend line
   - Current form indicator

5. **Bike maintenance dashboard**
   - Chain: 1,800 / 2,000 miles (progress bar at 90%)
   - Tires: 500 / 3,000 miles (progress bar at 16%)
   - Visual indicators with icons

## Success Metrics (How to Measure Ad Performance)

Once ad is running, track:
- **View-through rate** (how many watch all 15s)
- **Click-through rate** (how many click to TestFlight)
- **TestFlight signups** (conversions from ad)
- **Cost per install** (ad spend / new users)

Target benchmarks:
- View-through: 30-40% (good for 15s ad)
- Click-through: 2-5% (varies by platform)
- Cost per install: $2-5 (cycling niche)

## Questions for Agency

When you send this brief, ask them:

1. **Portfolio**: Can you share examples of app promo ads you've done?
2. **Approach**: Will you use stock footage, custom filming, or both?
3. **Revisions**: How many rounds of revisions are included?
4. **Platforms**: Can you deliver all formats listed (vertical, horizontal, square)?
5. **Timeline**: What's your estimated turnaround time?
6. **Music**: Is music licensing included in your quote?
7. **Voiceover**: Do you have voice talent, or should I source separately?
8. **Rights**: Will I own all final files and have unlimited usage rights?
